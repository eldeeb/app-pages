Sanad Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Sanad (“the app”, package com.linah.sanad) scans paper documents into PDFs and images, reads their text, and keeps them organized, right on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it.

Summary

Sanad does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any of your data. Your documents never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

No network access

Sanad does not have Android's INTERNET permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code, and it has no user accounts. The developer receives nothing from the app.

What the app accesses on your device

  • Scanning: Sanad uses Google's document scanner, which runs inside Google Play services on your device. Sanad itself has no camera permission: the scanner returns only the pages you choose to keep.
  • Importing: photos and PDFs you pick in the app, or share to Sanad from another app. Sanad has no permission to browse your storage; it only receives the files you give it.
  • Text recognition (OCR): the text on your pages is read on your device, by Google's on-device text recognition in Google Play services and by Tesseract inside the app. Nothing is uploaded.

Data stored on your device

Sanad keeps your documents in its private storage on your device: the page images, the recognized text, a search index, the titles, tags and folders, and your settings. You can delete documents in the app at any time, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

If you have turned on Android's own backup, the system may include this data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by Sanad, and the developer cannot access it.

When you protect a PDF with a password, the password is used only to encrypt that file. Sanad does not store it.

Purchases

If you buy Sanad Pro or leave a tip, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. Sanad never sees your payment details. To confirm a purchase, Sanad receives a purchase record from the Play Store app on your device (the product, an order ID and a purchase token). It keeps only whether Pro is active, encrypted in its private storage on your device, and never sends it to the developer.

Sharing

When you export a document (PDF, images, ZIP, one long image or text), Sanad passes the file to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. What happens to it after that is governed by that app's privacy policy. Sanad does not share data any other way.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
