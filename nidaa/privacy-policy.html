Nidaa Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Nidaa (“the app”, package com.linah.nidaa) is an offline prayer-times, Athan and Qibla app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

Nidaa does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet access: it does not request Android's INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or accounts.

Location

If you choose automatic location, the app asks for your device's location so it can calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It takes a single location fix when you ask for it, not continuous updates, and never runs in the background. Your coordinates are used only on your device and are saved only in the app's private local storage. Instead, you can pick your city from a list built into the app, and then no location permission is needed.

Nearby mosques. Only if you tap the “Nearby mosques” button, Nidaa asks your maps app (or your web browser, if no maps app is installed) to search for mosques around your saved place. It passes that app your saved coordinates and the word “mosque”. The search then happens in that app, under its own privacy policy; Nidaa itself still sends nothing, as it has no internet access.

Other permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, vibration and starting at boot: used to alert you at prayer times and to play the Athan.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): used only if you turn on “auto-silent during prayer”, to silence your phone during the prayer and then restore your setting.
  • Compass and accelerometer: read on the device to point the Qibla compass. The readings are not stored.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (city or coordinates, calculation method, alert preferences and language) are stored locally on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you have turned on Android's own backup, the system may include these settings in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by Nidaa.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
