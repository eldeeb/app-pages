ByteDiet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

ByteDiet (“the app”, package com.linah.bytediet) frees up storage by re-encoding your photos and videos into smaller files, right on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it.

Summary

ByteDiet does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any of your data. Everything happens on your device.

No network access

ByteDiet does not request Android's INTERNET permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code, and it has no user accounts. The developer receives nothing from the app.

What the app accesses on your device

To do its job, ByteDiet reads and changes data that never leaves your device:

  • Photos and videos: ByteDiet reads the media in the folders you choose, writes smaller versions, and depending on your settings either replaces the originals or saves the copies next to them. It keeps the date, location (GPS), camera details and favorite status already stored in your files and gallery, so your gallery looks the same afterwards. ByteDiet does not analyze the content of your photos or videos.
  • All files access: on Android 11 and later, ByteDiet asks for “All files access” (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). This is the only way Android lets an app replace media files in place, including from a background job, without asking you to confirm every file. ByteDiet uses this access only for the photos and videos in the folders you choose to scan.
  • Notifications: these show progress while files are being compressed.
  • Battery status: automatic compression runs only while the device is charging, and pauses when the battery is low.

Data stored on your device

ByteDiet keeps a small amount of data in its private storage on your device: your settings, such as quality, the folders to scan and excluded folders, and a record of the files it has processed, such as file paths, sizes and results. This record means it doesn't compress the same file twice and can show how much space you saved. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling does not undo compression that has already happened.

If you have turned on Android's own backup, the system may include these settings and records in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by ByteDiet, and the developer cannot access it.

Sharing

When you use “Compress & share”, ByteDiet makes a smaller copy of the photo or video you picked and passes it to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. What happens to it after that is governed by that app's privacy policy. ByteDiet does not share data any other way.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
